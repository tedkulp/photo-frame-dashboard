# Chore owner, days and deletion are dated, not overwritten

The week grid reads past days, so a chore's owner, days of the week and deletion are stored as dated changes that apply from the day they're made, and a deleted chore is hidden rather than removed; past days always show what was scheduled then. We rejected recomputing the past from the current chore list (simplest, but an edit could turn last Monday's hit into a miss) and storing each day's result at midnight (needs a scheduled job or a compute-on-first-read rule). Title, emoji and routine are overwritten, since nothing about past days depends on them.
